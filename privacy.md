Privacy Policy – Tiktok2Discord

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. About this App

Tiktok2Discord is an independent application that connects TikTok video notifications with a Discord server. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or Discord.

2. Information We Process

The application may process:

- TikTok public video information, such as video links, titles and publication dates.
- TikTok account information made available through the permissions granted by the account owner.
- Discord channel information required to send notifications.
- Video identifiers used to prevent duplicate notifications.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used only to detect newly published videos from the TikTok account authorized by its owner and send notifications to the configured Discord channel.

4. Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information. Notifications are shared only with members who can access the configured Discord channel.

5. Data Security and Retention

Access tokens and other credentials must be stored securely and must not be published. Video identifiers may be stored to prevent duplicate notifications.

6. Third-Party Services

The application relies on TikTok and Discord services. Their respective privacy policies apply to information processed by those services.
